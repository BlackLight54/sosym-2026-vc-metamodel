## Goal

Claim #7 uses the phrase "known anti-patterns," implying external provenance. The five anti-patterns are derived from the formalization, not from a published catalog.

## Acceptable risk rationale

The anti-patterns are structural consequences of the metamodel — they are "known" in the sense that the formalization makes them visible. The word "known" is defensible as "identified by our analysis" rather than "previously published." The adversarial reviewer may probe this, but the defense is straightforward.
