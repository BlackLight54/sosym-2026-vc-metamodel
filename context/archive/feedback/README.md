# Feedback Archive

Meeting notes and reviewer comments, preserved for historical context.

- **Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD_description.md` (e.g., `2026-03-15_advisor_meeting.md`).
- **Contents:** Raw notes, action items extracted, who was present, decisions made.
- Feedback that generates active tasks should produce todo files in `context/todos/`. The notes here are the source record.
- Do not put research findings or cut text here; those have their own archive directories.
