- **Method (one paragraph):** For every research question and binding claim, this audit records the *type* of backing behind it, drawn from four categories: (a) corpus-verified sources, the 74 adversarially verified proto-SLR entries; (b) primary regulatory instruments, the version-pinned normative texts (ARF, eIDAS 2, GDPR, CIR 2025/848, ETSI, W3C VCDM, national decree, plus enforcement precedent); (c) own artifacts and measurements, A-001..A-005 and the evaluation harness output; and (d) only our own argument, prose audits and syntheses with no reproducing artifact or external citation. A fixed rubric grades each item: **SOLID** = delivered and backed by an own artifact, a primary instrument, or the corpus with no open evidential item; **ADEQUATE** = delivered but the backing is own-argument-grade, or a scheduled open item could still shift the claim wording; **THIN** = the backing rests on a stale or absent source, or the claim may not survive a pending re-verification. The rubric is applied mechanically, not by feel. One blanket confidence limiter applies throughout: per corpus map §7, all corpus characterizations rest on abstracts, index records, and verification notes, not full texts, so every grade that leans on how a corpus item is characterized (novelty deltas, quote-level claims) is capped until full-text pulls are done.

## Thin-spot list (ranked by residual, uncovered risk)

Ranking rule: an uncovered spot (pipeline coverage NONE) outranks an equally impactful covered one, because nothing is scheduled to close it.

1. **K-006 expressiveness rests on a stale ARF version (highest impact, covered but not done).** Sections cite ARF v2.7.3; current is v2.9.0. ARF-C1..C8 are unverified against v2.9.0 §5.4/§7.4, and the expressiveness classification could shift, changing both the K-006 claim wording and F5 content (R4, O8). This is the one thin spot that can move a *delivered* claim. **Action:** O-EUTHREAT re-verification, re-run the expressiveness classification against v2.9.0, re-pin the ARF bib entry (S-034). **Covered by:** gate 1 (blocks gates 2 and 3); Tier-1 mandatory.
2. **Non-web-PKI absence undercuts RQ0 Leg 3 and the taxonomy's PKI grounding (uncovered).** Corpus axis d is entirely web PKI/TLS. eIDAS 1 qualified certificates, S/MIME, code signing, and enterprise PKI, which are the arguably closer B2B/B2G relatives, are absent (corpus §7 gap 7, §4 honesty obligation). Leg 3 and the taxonomy therefore lean on the *wrong PKI sub-domain* for the target ecosystem. This sits at #2 because **NONE** of the planned gates or todos owns the extension. **Action:** a non-web-PKI targeted-search extension arm, or an explicit, prominent limitation statement if the arm is declined. **Covered by:** NONE (corpus §7 gap 7 names it as "limitation or targeted search extension"; no gate or todo is assigned).
3. **Whole-corpus characterizations rest on abstracts, not full texts (broad, partially covered).** Every corpus-backed claim, RQ0's legs, RQ1's Sroor novelty delta, and the taxonomy carriers, is characterized from abstracts and verification notes (corpus §7 gap 12). Load-bearing verbatim quotes (Schmidt "ill-defined", Bochnia "largely unexplored", Sroor "visually and informally") are unverified against source. **Action:** full-text pull of the load-bearing items before the claims that depend on them freeze. **Covered by:** partially, gate 8 currency sweep and the camera-ready quote-precision obligation; no dedicated full-text-pull todo exists for the pre-freeze load-bearing items, so the risk is live during drafting.
4. **K-005 coverage and K-010 necessity are own-argument-grade, no reproducing artifact (covered).** K-005 is a prose audit; K-010 is by its own description an argument over the other claims. Neither is tool-checked. **Action:** the F4 coverage figure formalizes K-005; the §5.3 external-validity paragraph (D-013) discharges K-010's open leg. **Covered by:** T11 (F4) and gate 3 (§5.3).
5. **K-004 detection breadth and the O3 synthetic-evaluation attack (covered).** Scalability rests on current instances; anti-patterns are hand-crafted. **Action:** structurally diverse instance generator plus measurement re-run adds the empirical leg. **Covered by:** AF02 (Q-007, D-021); O3 sourcing already threaded through §5.1.

## Verdict

The paper's core modeling contribution is well backed and the audit finds it defensible: RQ1, RQ2, and RQ3 grade SOLID because each is carried by an own artifact (A-001 metamodel, A-005 harness, A-002/A-003/A-004) and, for RQ3, by primary regulatory instruments including a conflict the regulator documents in its own text plus a minimality ablation; eight of ten binding claims grade SOLID or ADEQUATE on delivered evidence. The exposure is concentrated in two places. First, the evaluation leg that touches the moving standard: K-006 is THIN because it rests on ARF v2.7.3 while v2.9.0 is current, and it is the only thin spot that can change a delivered claim's wording. Second, the relevance and taxonomy story (RQ0 plus the not-yet-promised design-error taxonomy), which is structurally own-synthesis, the corpus states plainly that RQ0 cannot be supported by a direct citation, and whose X.509 grounding covers only web PKI, the wrong sub-domain for the B2B/B2G target, with no pipeline session owning the extension. The 2026-07-13 corpus work is real but mostly reinforces *positioning and objection rebuttals* (no single rival, formal-below/informal-at, the O3 sourcing) rather than the direct truth of any claim, so it should not be read as upgrading claim backing. Top three actions: (1) run the O-EUTHREAT re-verification against ARF v2.9.0 to stabilize K-006 and F5 (gate 1); (2) decide the non-web-PKI question, either add a targeted-search extension arm or commit to an explicit limitation, since nothing currently owns it; (3) schedule full-text pulls of the load-bearing corpus quotes before the RQ0, Sroor-novelty, and taxonomy claims freeze, rather than deferring all quote precision to camera-ready.
