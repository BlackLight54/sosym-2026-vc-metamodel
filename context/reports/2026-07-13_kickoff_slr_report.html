Consequence for related work: there is no single rival to beat; the gap is assembled from partial neighbors, each strong on exactly one dimension. That is a more defensible novelty position than displacing one competitor, and it decides how §6 of the paper gets restructured.

On the deployment side the spectrum is equally clear: the only ecosystem actually being built is the consumer-facing EUDI wallet (B2C); B2B/B2G exists as pilots and prototypes (EBSI education pilots, a supply-chain prototype, the Siemens/Deutsche Bahn device-lifecycle prototype) with exactly one production counterpoint, British Columbia's OrgBook business registry. The organizational-credential building blocks are being standardized right now (ETSI TS 119 471 for QEAA policy; vLEI for organizational identity; Catena-X and Gaia-X for data-space trust), which is precisely the „world state” RQ0 needs: components in place, the ecosystem itself still hypothetical.

3What the literature tells us: five load-bearing inferences

These are the readings that should influence decisions. Each names its evidence; where the step beyond the sources is ours, it is marked.

Inference 1 · the definitional gap is real and 6 years old

Nobody has defined the VC ecosystem concept, and the field keeps saying so

An unbroken chain: Schmidt 2021 (SSI ecosystem „rapidly changing and ill-defined”), Soltani 2021 (surveys it while conceding the definition is open), Schardong & Custódio 2022 (defining SSI is the primary open conceptual problem), Laatikainen 2021 (SSI ecosystems as emergent, prospective socio-technical ecosystems), Richter & Anke 2026 (consolidated framework still missing).

So: the advisor's instinct that the ecosystem concept „megér egy definíciós kísérletet” is confirmed as a citable gap, not just an intuition. The paper can and should make the definitional attempt, with Otto 2022 (data spaces as designed ecosystems) and Durumeric 2013 (an ecosystem operationalized as a measurable object) as the two construction templates.

Inference 2 · pre-deployment error visibility is proven

Design errors in credential ecosystems are visible before deployment, demonstrably

The EUDI build-out is the live experiment: the 2024 cryptographers' feedback named linkability failures in the ARF before rollout; Abellan Alvarez 2026 is a peer-reviewed privacy analysis of the architecture itself; Burmeister 2026 found a second-order design error formally, in a spec with no deployment yet; Fraser & Schneider 2025 document an 8-year gap between a spec shipping and its flaw being found.

So: RQ0's premise („errors can be anticipated from the current state of the world”) is not speculative: anticipation is already happening at protocol level. The paper's claim is lifting it to ecosystem level. This also fixes claim discipline: everything is worded as errors visible in specifications and pilots, because zero post-deployment VC evidence exists anywhere.

Inference 3 · the PKI lesson is about enforcement, not warnings

PKI errors were anticipated in 2000; anticipation alone did not prevent them. Machine-checkable rules did the correcting

Ellison & Schneier named the design risks before mass deployment (2000); the incidents happened anyway (DigiNotar 2011, documented in the Fox-IT report; 1300+ incidents in Serrano 2019). What measurably reduced misissuance was compiling governance rules into machine-checkable predicates: ZLint (Kumar 2018), plus institutional remediation (Grindal 2025). Hadan 2021 adds that expert intuition about failures diverges from what incident data shows.

Our inference This is the strongest single argument for the paper's mechanism: a taxonomy informs, but the PKI history says checkable constraints are what change outcomes. The paper offers exactly that, one level up: ecosystem-level lint. The taxonomy (advisor's closing element) and the formal machinery (existing contribution) argue for each other.

Inference 4 · the nearest neighbor is informal, not formal

The novelty threat is Sroor 2022 and the vLEI EGF, not the protocol verifiers

The formal-methods line (Braun, Tamarin, ZLint, Mao) works below ecosystem scope and is complementary; each analyzes one protocol, artifact, or codebase. The items occupying the paper's scope are informal: Sroor's visual domain-specific models of multi-source SSI governance, and GLEIF's vLEI EGF, the nearest existing „metamodel” claim, which is documentary prose-and-policy on the ToIP governance metamodel.

So: the differentiation sentences to sharpen are „visual vs checkable” (Sroor) and „documentary vs analyzable” (vLEI EGF). The vLEI contrast is a gift: a real, operating organizational-credential ecosystem whose governance framework literally calls itself metamodel-based, yet supports no automated analysis. It is the running contrast the related-work section should be built around.

Inference 5 · multi-source causation is the PKI-confirmed frame

Credential-infrastructure failures are multi-source, which is the paper's core premise seen historically

Lopez 2005 attributes PKI failure to interacting technical, economic, legal, and social causes; Grindal 2025 treats governance as a first-class causal layer in the web PKI; Brubaker 2014 shows specification ambiguity alone produces divergent implementations.

So: the thesis's „independently enacted governance sources impose conflicting constraints” is not a construction of ours; the predecessor ecosystem's failure literature already talks this way. This slots directly into the motivation as the historical backing for cross-layer, multi-source analysis.

4The RQ0 verdict: unclaimed ground

No item in the verified corpus characterizes the design-error threat for future VC-based B2B/B2G ecosystems, and none defines the ecosystem concept in consolidated form. The closest items (Gruener's present-tense SSI threat model, Sharif's single-ecosystem EUDI threat model, Slamanig's recurring-failure-pattern essay) each stop one step short. What the corpus supplies instead is every premise, each fully citable:

Leg 1 · hypothetical & unsettled

The definitional gap chain (Inference 1) plus deployment evidence: pilots, prototypes, one production counterpoint. The emergence is a hypothesis, and the corpus lets the paper say so honestly.

Leg 2 · errors already visible

Pre-deployment error visibility in the one ecosystem being built (Inference 2).

Leg 3 · the precedent ecosystem

A comparable credential trust infrastructure with a documented, classified, partly anticipated error history (Inference 3).

↓
The paper's own move

Synthesizing the three legs into an anticipatory design-error taxonomy for B2B/B2G VC ecosystems, backed by the formal machinery that makes the errors detectable. The synthesis is claimed as a contribution; no citation is stretched to cover it.

5The X.509 precedent holds

Anticipation precedent

Ellison & Schneier 2000 named design-level PKI risks before mass deployment; several materialized as documented incidents.

Taxonomizability precedent

Serrano 2019 (1300+ incidents, explicit taxonomy) and Hadan 2021 (failure dimensions; intuition diverges from incident data): errors of this kind submit to systematic classification grounded in history, not speculation.

Candidate error classes with named carrier studies: misissuance (Kumar 2018), revocation and status failure (Liu 2015: the class with the most direct VC analog in status lists), verifier-side validation misuse (Georgiev 2012), specification ambiguity (Brubaker 2014), single-point-of-trust compromise (Fox-IT 2012), governance failure (Grindal 2025), design/reality mismatch (Gutmann 2002), multi-source causation (Lopez 2005); Clark & van Oorschot 2013 as the canonical systematization. No existing PKI-to-SSI lessons paper was found; Slamanig 2025 comes closest but draws on anonymous-credentials history, so it gets cited and distinguished, not displaced.

7Ideas worth taking into the paper

  • „Ecosystem-level lint” as the mechanism story. ZLint compiled two governance sources into checkable certificate rules and measurably reduced misissuance; the paper does the same move one level up, over partial graph models. A one-sentence lineage that any reviewer immediately places.
  • The definitional attempt as a mini-contribution. Combine Otto's designed-ecosystem stance with Laatikainen's socio-technical framing and Durumeric's operationalization: define the VC ecosystem as the object our three layers model, then use that definition to scope the taxonomy. Fills a 6-year documented gap at low cost.
  • vLEI as the running contrast. An operating ecosystem, self-described as metamodel-based, zero automated analysis: every claim about „documentary vs analyzable” governance can point at it.
  • Taxonomy dimensions from the metamodel itself. Classify each error class by which layer or layer-pair it spans; classes that do not map get named as out-of-model honestly. The taxonomy then advertises the metamodel instead of standing beside it.
  • Franz & Shams as the industrial anecdote. A Siemens/Deutsche Bahn prototype with a practitioner-named design friction (device world vs web technology): one verified grey source that makes the B2B threat concrete without overclaiming.
  • Candidate new anti-pattern instances from the corpus: the Catena-X managed-wallet tension (custody vs self-sovereignty in CX-0149), trusted-list governance failure modes (Termont & Esteves 2026), and the mandate/representation slot (Flamini 2025) as a forward-looking error class; each a possible constraint-catalogue or future-work item.
