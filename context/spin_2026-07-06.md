## 1. Thesis

Verbatim from CLAUDE.md:

> **Core claim:** Verifiable credential ecosystem design requires a multi-layer metamodel to capture constraints that span domain semantics, credential structure, and format-specific capabilities.
> **Mechanism:** Three-layer metamodel (domain-level claim properties, abstract credential schemas, format-specific representations) grounded in W3C VCDM 2.0, with cross-layer constraints formalized as graph predicates in the Refinery partial graph modeling framework.

So-what: without such a metamodel, independently enacted governance frameworks can impose formally conflicting constraints on one ecosystem, and design errors spanning multiple layers go undetected by single-layer inspection.

## 2. Research questions

- **RQ0 (relevance) `[proposed]`.** Do VC-based B2B/B2G credential ecosystems exist today, and if they emerge as hypothesized, can the design errors they will face be anticipated from the current deployment spectrum and the documented error history of predecessor infrastructures (X.509/PKI)? Adopted per D-020 (advisor mandate, 2026-07-06); answered by the proto-SLR corpus map (`context/slr/corpus_map.md`) and closed by the design-error taxonomy (§4). Corpus-checked 2026-07-13: no verified item characterizes the threat or defines the consolidated ecosystem concept, so the paper claims RQ0 as its own synthesis over three citable legs (definitional gap + emergence evidence; pre-deployment errors in the EUDI B2C build-out; X.509 anticipation precedent). Scope discipline from the corpus: error claims narrow to "errors visible in specifications and pilots", no post-deployment VC evidence exists. Wording stays `[proposed]` for Martin's approval; objection O13 (M-010) carries the speculativeness attack and its committed rebuttal.
- **RQ1 (formalization).** Can the constraints that independently enacted governance sources impose on a verifiable-credential ecosystem, spanning domain semantics, credential structure, and format capabilities, be captured in a single formal metamodel? Answered by K-001, K-002, K-003 on the coverage (K-005) and expressiveness (K-006) evaluation axes.
- **RQ2 (detection).** Can design errors that span multiple layers of such a model, and are therefore invisible to single-layer inspection, be detected automatically, including on incomplete designs? Answered by K-004, K-007, K-009 on the anti-pattern axis, in both analysis modalities (D-008).
- **RQ3 (instantiation).** Do the governance frameworks binding the EU digital-identity stack impose formally conflicting cross-layer constraints on a concrete credential ecosystem, and can the formalization localize the conflict to a minimal constraint set? Answered by K-008 and K-010 through the headline results and the G0–G7 ablation (A-004).

RQ1-RQ3 wording approved 2026-07-13 (D-019, closing gate 5); RQ0 adoption is D-020. RQ2 and RQ3 are calibrated to what M-011 backs: detection sufficiency and structural prerequisite, not necessity (K-010 calibration, M-013).
