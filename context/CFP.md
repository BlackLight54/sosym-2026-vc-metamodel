## About the journal

SoSyM describes itself as a bi-monthly international journal, published in English, focused on theoretical and practical issues in the development and application of modeling languages and techniques. It aims to publish work that advances the understanding of modeling foundations, provides rigorous analyses of modeling experiences, and introduces scalable modeling techniques. The Springer page states the scope as theoretical and practical issues in the development and application of software and system modeling languages, techniques, and methods, such as the Unified Modeling Language.

**Editors-in-chief:** Marsha Chechik (University of Toronto), Benoit Combemale (University of Rennes), Bernhard Rumpe (RWTH Aachen University).

**Publication model:** Hybrid (subscription with open-access option). Impact factor 3.0 (2025), 5-year 2.9 (2025), per the Springer journal page.

## Submission requirements (sosym.org/submission and Springer submission guidelines)

- Continuous submission; no deadlines. Publication is first come, first served, with possible fast-track exceptions.
- No page restrictions. Papers must include an abstract, keywords, and literature references.
- Abstract length: 150 to 250 words (Springer guidelines).
- Submitted papers: font no smaller than 11pt, 1.5 line spacing.
- LaTeX: Springer Nature LaTeX template with the `[iicol]` formatting option (Springer guidelines); sosym.org describes the template as recommended, not required. Springer typesets accepted papers into the final layout.
- Complete editable source files (text, style files, figures) must accompany every submission and revision, or the article is not sent to review.
- References: numbered citations in square brackets; DOIs as full links when available; journal abbreviations per the ISSN List of Title Word Abbreviations.
- Figures: EPS for vector art, TIFF for halftones; line art 1200 dpi, halftones 300 dpi, combination art 600 dpi; Helvetica or Arial lettering, 2 to 3 mm.
- Headings: decimal numbering, no more than three levels.

## Review process (sosym.org/review_process, sosym.org/submission, Springer submission guidelines)

- Single-blind: "This journal follows a single-blind reviewing procedure" (Springer submission guidelines).
- At least three reviewers per paper.
- Timeline targets: editors assigned within 2 weeks of submission; reviews within 8 weeks of reviewer acceptance; editor recommendation within 1 week of receiving reviews; authors notified within 1 week of the recommendation; overall goal of contacting authors within 12 weeks of initial submission. The editors-in-chief aim to obtain final reviews within sixteen weeks.
- Decision categories: acceptance; accepted with minor modifications; conditionally accepted with major changes (second review round); rejection.
- A manuscript can undergo a major revision only once. If major revisions are needed again after a major revision, the manuscript is rejected.

## Policies (sosym.org/submission)

- No simultaneous submission: papers submitted to SoSyM must not be simultaneously submitted to other journals or conferences.
- Self-plagiarism thresholds: publication is prohibited when 75% or more of the content previously appeared in non-journal sources, or 40% or more in journal sources.
