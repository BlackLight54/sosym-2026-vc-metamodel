## Problem

The abstract says "surfaces two cross-layer design errors." The two headline errors (governance conflict and cross-credential predicate gap) are constructed by design in the running example — they were placed there to demonstrate the metamodel's detection capability. "Surfaces" implies empirical discovery of previously unknown errors, which is misleading.

## Source

- X02 (Claim-Evidence Audit): Hedging issue H-3

## Goal

Replace "surfaces" with "demonstrates detection of" (or similar verb that conveys demonstrated capability rather than empirical discovery).

## Suggested fix

In `sections/00_abstract.md`, find the phrase "surfaces two cross-layer design errors" and replace with "demonstrates detection of two cross-layer design errors."

## Acceptance criteria

- Abstract no longer uses "surfaces" for the headline results
- Replacement verb conveys demonstrated capability, not empirical discovery
- Sentence remains grammatically natural
