The three research questions proposed in spin §2 are **approved as worded**: RQ1
(formalization), RQ2 (detection), RQ3 (instantiation). They keep their numbering and sit under
the new RQ0 adopted in [[D-020 rq0-relevance-and-error-taxonomy]]; RQ0 precedes them in the
paper's question set.

## Rationale
The spin wording was calibrated to what M-011 backs: RQ2 and RQ3 claim detection sufficiency
and structural prerequisite, not necessity (the K-010 calibration in M-013). Rewording risks
re-inflating claims the calibration deliberately narrowed. The advisor asked for an RQ0 above
them; he did not object to the RQ1-RQ3 substance.

## Commits the paper to
The spin §2 `[proposed]` labels on RQ1-RQ3 are lifted. Any later material change to RQ wording
reopens this decision explicitly. RQ0's own final wording stays `[proposed]` until the
proto-SLR corpus grounds it (see [[D-020 rq0-relevance-and-error-taxonomy]]).
