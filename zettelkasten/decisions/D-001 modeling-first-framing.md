The paper is framed as a **metamodeling contribution**, not an error-detection tool paper. The
three-layer metamodel and cross-layer constraint formalization are the contribution; error detection,
DSE, and the headline conflicts are *consequences* that demonstrate the metamodel's value, not the
headline framing.

## Rationale
Targets an MDE audience and foregrounds the durable artifact (the metamodel) over a single application.
Keeps the motivation section ending on a problem statement, with results deferred to evaluation.
