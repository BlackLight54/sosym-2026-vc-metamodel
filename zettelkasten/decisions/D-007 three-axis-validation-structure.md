§5 is organized by three validation axes — coverage (vs W3C), expressiveness (vs EU regulation), error
detection (vs anti-patterns) — plus a quantitative scalability measurement with a fixed schema (RQs,
domains, setup, results, threats) targeting ≥2 pages.

## Rationale
Three axes map cleanly onto the three contributions and give reviewers a legible evaluation skeleton.
The fixed measurement schema pre-empts "where are the numbers" objections without overstating empirical
scope.
