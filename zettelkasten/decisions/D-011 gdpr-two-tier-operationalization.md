eIDAS ARF is treated as a **hard** format mandate; GDPR data minimization is treated as **operationally
binding** — not a textual format mandate but a principle the paper operationalizes as a predicate-proof
requirement, backed by the Hungarian DPA enforcement precedent.

## Rationale
Grading the two sources pre-empts the objection that the conflict is manufactured by over-reading GDPR.
Even under a conservative reading the operational interpretation is defensible, so the conflict holds.
