The paper targets a **model-driven engineering** venue and audience, not a formal-methods one. Register,
explanation floor, and reviewer personas are calibrated to MDE: metamodel definitions and graph
predicates, not theorem–proof structure.

## Rationale
The contribution is a metamodel and a modeling methodology; the formal apparatus (Refinery) is borrowed,
not the novelty. MDE reviewers value the modeling choices and their motivation over proof depth.
