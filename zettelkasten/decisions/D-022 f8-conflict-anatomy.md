Figure **F8 (conflict anatomy)** enters the committed figure set, alongside F1, F2, F3, F5,
F7, F9. The spin default excluded it; the ruling reverses the default.

## Rationale
F8 carries the D-014 framing visually in the motivation section: the conflict's anatomy as the
regulator's own documents state it, before the running example instantiates it. That pre-empts
rank-4 objection O5 ("conflict manufactured by over-reading GDPR") at first sight instead of
in prose three sections later. Figures carry the argument (CLAUDE.md doctrine); the anatomy of
a cross-source conflict is exactly the kind of claim prose carries poorly.

## Commits the paper to
One more figure to produce (tracked with the T11 backlog); a §3 motivation placement near the
ARF §7.4.3.5.1 anchor quote; a spec via `skills/draft_figure_design` before Stage 4 prose
starts.
