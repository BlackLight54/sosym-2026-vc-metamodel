The advisor's boxed probe "IPAR 5.0 ? mint spin" is **rejected as the paper's framing**.
Industry 5.0 remains a **probe inside proto-SLR axis (c)** (QEAA, Data Spaces, organizational
credentials); the ruling is revisited at SLR integration only if the corpus shows a
substantial Industry-5.0-and-VC literature.

## Rationale
Argued from the vault, not taste. M-012's Heilmeier "who cares" cell is regulator-driven:
ecosystem designers under eIDAS 2 deadlines and the regulators whose own framework documents
the conflict. M-009 and D-014 rest the paper's relevance on regulation in force and the
regulator's own text. An Industry 5.0 spin would replace that documented leg with a projected
industrial adoption wave, which is exactly the unproven hypothesis RQ0 exists to handle
honestly. It would also open a frame-evidence mismatch on O3 (motivation promises industrial
ecosystems, evaluation delivers an EU public-sector subsidy example) and widen O10 (broader
promised applicability with no new evidence), while no M-011 row backs any industry claim.
The corpus base is thin: one arXiv position paper on smart legal ecosystems is the only
Industry 5.0 hit in the gap-analysis archive; the neighboring hits are Industry 4.0
manufacturing and data-space items already covered by axis (c).

## Commits the paper to
No Industry 5.0 framing in any pipeline artifact. Axis (c) finders probe the Industry 4.0/5.0
and VC/data-spaces line explicitly; the corpus map reports what exists. Revisit condition:
axis (c) returns a substantial, verified I5.0-VC literature at integration, in which case the
question goes back to Martin with evidence.
