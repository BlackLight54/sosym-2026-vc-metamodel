Validation scopes the CORE-DISC properties deliberately: **Consistency** is primary, **Diverse** and
**Scalable** are addressed (DSE diversity guarantee; scalability measurement), **Realistic** is out of
scope (no empirical practitioner study).

## Rationale
Claiming all properties would overreach; naming which are in and out of scope is honest and pre-empts
the "no user study" objection by declaring it a limitation rather than a hidden gap.
