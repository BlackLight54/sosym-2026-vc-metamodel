The paper adopts an **RQ0 on relevance**, preceding RQ1-RQ3, and a **design-error taxonomy as
its closing element**. Interim RQ0 wording, `[proposed]` until the proto-SLR corpus grounds it:

> **RQ0 (relevance).** Do VC-based B2B/B2G credential ecosystems exist today, and if they
> emerge as hypothesized, can the design errors they will face be anticipated from the current
> deployment spectrum and the documented error history of predecessor infrastructures
> (X.509/PKI)?

## Rationale
Advisor consultation 2026-07-06: VC-based ecosystems do not exist yet in B2B/B2G contexts;
their emergence is a hypothesis; the ecosystem concept itself deserves a definitional attempt.
The paper must justify that the problem will need addressing. The taxonomy closes the arc: IF
such ecosystems emerge, the design errors are foreseeable from the current state of the world.
Motivation comes from the **X.509 precedent** (a documented PKI design and deployment error
history), not from our own examples (the advisor's "no navel-gazing" constraint); the taxonomy
stays in sync with the two headline examples (vertical income conflict, horizontal floor-area
gap) and is systematic rather than anecdotal.

## Commits the paper to
Spin §2 gains RQ0 above RQ1-RQ3; the §4 delta gains the taxonomy as closing element; the §6 O3
rebuttal row gains the X.509 precedent leg. A new K-note and M-011 row for the taxonomy claim
are created at SLR integration, `[proposed]` and backed by the corpus map; until then no prose
promises the taxonomy (no promise a blank M-011 evidence cell cannot back). The proto-SLR is
the evidence instrument for RQ0; if the corpus cannot ground RQ0, the claim narrows rather
than inflates.
