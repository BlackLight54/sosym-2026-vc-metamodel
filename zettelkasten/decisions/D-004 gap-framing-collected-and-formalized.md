Frame the contribution as **collecting and formalizing** constraints that were previously informal or
dispersed, not merely observing that they are "scattered across" sources. The novelty is the
formalization that makes joint checking possible, confirmed by the gap analysis (core novelty stands).

## Rationale
"Scattered" understates the work and invites the "just engineering" objection. "Collected and
formalized as graph predicates" names the actual contribution and supports the necessity argument.
