**W3C, 2025, Recommendation.** Defines a verifiable credential as a tamper-evident set of issuer claims
about subjects; standardizes the subject–property–value structural vocabulary and issuer/holder/verifier
roles.

## Relation to this work
The grounding spec. The metamodel maps every metaclass and capability predicate to a VCDM concept
(soundness) and excludes three usage-time families (completeness). VCDM is single-layer structural
conformance; it does not span domain semantics or format capabilities.
