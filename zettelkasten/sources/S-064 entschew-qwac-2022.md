**Entschew, Hall, Bailey, Nguyen, 2022, Datenschutz und Datensicherheit (DuD).**
Non-web-PKI carrier for the cross-credential expressiveness-gap class: documents that the QWAC instrument defined in eIDAS 1 (Reg 910/2014) has, since 2014, persistently failed to deliver usable website-identity assurance in practice (browsers never meaningfully surfaced its qualified-identity attributes, adoption stalled, and it collapsed into overlap with EV), evidencing an attribute-expressiveness and relying-party-display failure of the credential.
