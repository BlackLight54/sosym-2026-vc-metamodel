**Commission Implementing Regulations under eIDAS 2, three adopted batches.** Verified primary (EC announcement pages + EUR-Lex, 3-0 vote).

- **Batch 1 (4 Dec 2024, OJ):** 2024/2977 (PID + EAA), 2024/2979 (integrity/core functionalities), 2024/2980 (notifications/ecosystem), 2024/2981 (certification), 2024/2982 (protocols and interfaces).
- **Batch 2 (adopted 6 May, published 7 May 2025):** 2025/846 (cross-border identity matching), 2025/847 (security breach notification), **2025/848 (relying-party registration)** → [[S-035 cir-2025-848-rp-registration]], 2025/849 (list of certified wallets).
- **Batch 3 (published 30 Jul 2025, in force ≈19 Aug 2025):** eight regulations (seven trust-services + one EAA specifications). **Open:** the exact 2025/xxxx OJ numbers and subjects of this round were not retrieved and must be located on EUR-Lex before citation.

## Relation to this work
Grounds the **moving-target regulation** threat ([[M-009 threats-to-applicability]]): the stack moved across at least three batches in eight months, and the verifier-side regime (2025/848) is in force but only *applicable* from 24 Dec 2026, after a typical mid-2026 submission. The paper should state regulatory currency as a dated snapshot. Batches 2 and 3 also broaden the governance catalogue beyond format mandates ([[Q-008 broaden-governance-catalog]]).
