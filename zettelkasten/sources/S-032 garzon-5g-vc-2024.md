**Garzón et al., 2024.** Prototypes a 5G Service-Based Architecture that uses verifiable credentials in
place of X.509 certificates and OAuth2 access tokens, with a hierarchical trust-delegation model among
issuers.

## Relation to this work
A sector-specific (telecommunications) VC-ecosystem exemplar cited as state of the art in the precursor
([[S-027 own-tdk-thesis-mda]]). Its takeaway motivates the paper: ecosystem design is shaped not only by
a single claim's properties but by the structures and hierarchies credentials form across issuers —
exactly the cross-credential interdependence the metamodel targets.
