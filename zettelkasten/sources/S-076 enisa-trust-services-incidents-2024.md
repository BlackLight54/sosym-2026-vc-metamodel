**ENISA, 2025, ENISA CIRAS annual report series.**
The mandatory aggregation of trust-service security incidents reported by national supervisory bodies under eIDAS, giving the documented failure history (root causes, outages, and human errors) of qualified and non-qualified trust services rather than a framework tutorial. It grounds the governance-conflict class in a primary agency record of how the supervisory regime behind the qualified-provider trusted lists actually fails.
