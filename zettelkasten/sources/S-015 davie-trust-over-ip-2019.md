**Davie, Gisolfi et al., 2019, IEEE Comm. Standards.** The ToIP four-layer reference stack combining
technical and governance layers for decentralized trust.

## Relation to this work
The best-known layered SSI model, but its layers are **informal** (gap RQ2). Differentiated: ToIP
describes layers narratively; the present work formalizes cross-layer constraints as graph predicates
with machine-checkable conflict detection.
