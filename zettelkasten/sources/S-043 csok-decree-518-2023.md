**Government Decree 518/2023 (Hungary).** Prescribes the housing-subsidy eligibility rule the running
example formalizes: minimum property floor areas of 40/50/60/70/80 m² for one through five-or-more
children.

## Relation to this work
The domain-rule source of constraint **C4** (cross-credential value dependency,
`property_area ≥ min_area(num_children)`), the arithmetic rule spanning two credentials that drives
Headline 2 ([[C-026 cross-credential-predicate-gap-horizontal]]). Grounds the claim that C4 is
regulation-derived, not invented. Cited in §3 as `[@noauthor_5182023_2023]`.
