**Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation), 2016.** Art. 5(1)(c): personal data
shall be "adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which they
are processed" (data minimization).

## Relation to this work
The third governance source of the paper's core triad (with W3C VCDM and the eIDAS ARF), and the origin
of constraint C6: minimization is operationalized as a predicate-proof format-capability requirement
([[C-023 gdpr-data-minimization]]), graded *operationally binding* rather than a hard textual mandate
([[C-024 two-tier-governance-framing]]), with the [[S-042 naih-minimization-fine-2020]] enforcement
precedent as empirical defense. Already cited in the sections as `[@gdpr]`; this note makes the
instrument retrievable alongside the other governance sources.
