**Jackson, 2012.** Alloy: bounded model finding over relational specifications via SAT-based
enumeration within a fixed scope.

## Relation to this work
The principal alternative formalism. Differentiated: Alloy operates on fully concrete instances and
re-encodes the whole problem per candidate, with no representation of open design decisions; Refinery's
four-valued partial semantics distinguish definite violations from open choices and reason
incrementally. Also the natural target for the portability question.
