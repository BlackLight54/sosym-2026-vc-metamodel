**Famelis, Salay, Chechik, 2012.** Formalizes reasoning under structural uncertainty in MDE: a single
partial model represents a set of possible concrete models.

## Relation to this work
The conceptual antecedent of Refinery's four-valued partial-model semantics. Grounds the claim that the
designer can reason over incomplete credential designs before all schema elements are known.
