**Atkinson & Kühne, 2001 (and 2008).** Foundational multi-level metamodeling: model elements spanning
more than two metalevels, eliminating accidental complexity in deep classification via instantiation
and potency.

## Relation to this work
The tradition the present metamodel **departs from**: its layers are independently governed concern
spaces connected by coverage/capability constraints, not instantiation; constraints are graph
predicates, not potency annotations (differentiation D5).
