**Marussy et al., 2024.** Partial graph modeling framework: design specifications as partial models,
graph predicates as a first-class constraint language, four-valued (Belnap) semantics, diverse model
generation guaranteed.

## Relation to this work
The solver infrastructure. The entire formalization (metamodel, cross-layer predicates) is a standard
Refinery specification, inheriting its generation guarantees and four-valued reasoning over incomplete
designs.
