**Terbu et al., 2026 (IETF draft).** SD-JWT-VC: hash-based selective disclosure for JWT verifiable
credentials.

## Relation to this work
The eIDAS-ARF-mandated representative format. Supports selective disclosure but **not** predicate
proofs, so it cannot satisfy the GDPR-operationalized privacy requirement on IncomeCred — the format
side of Headline 1. Used as the representative mandated format throughout.
