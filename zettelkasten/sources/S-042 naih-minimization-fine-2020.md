**Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH), 2020.** Enforcement decision fining a bank for copying
entire pregnancy booklets when only a threshold/status check was required.

## Relation to this work
The empirical defense of the GDPR operationalization: a DPA treated disclosing an underlying document as
a minimization violation when only a derived boolean was needed — directly analogous to requiring
predicate-proof capability for the income threshold check (C6). This is what lets the paper claim the
operationalization is defensible "even under a conservative reading"
([[D-011 gdpr-two-tier-operationalization]]). Cited in the §3 motivation footnote as
`[@noauthor_naih_2020]`.
