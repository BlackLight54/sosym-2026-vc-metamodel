**Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 of 11 April 2024**, amending Reg. (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS) to establish the
European Digital Identity Framework. The statute at the top of the EU governance stack the paper
formalizes: **Art. 5a(16)(b)** imposes the wallet's unlinkability duty where user identification is not
needed; **Art. 5b** governs relying parties, including the **Art. 5b(3)** request-time data-minimization
duty; its empowerments are the legal bases of the implementing acts (CIR 2024/2977 dual issuance,
CIR 2025/848 RP registration, and the rest of the three batches).

## Relation to this work
The primary legal instrument behind both horns of the real-world vertical conflict
([[C-034 statutory-unlinkability-gap]]): the statutory unlinkability and minimization duties on one
