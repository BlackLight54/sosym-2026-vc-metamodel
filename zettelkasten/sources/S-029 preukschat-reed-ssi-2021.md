**Preukschat & Reed, 2021, Manning** (ISBN 978-1-61729-659-8). The standard practitioner reference for
self-sovereign identity: decentralized digital identity, DIDs, and verifiable credentials.

## Relation to this work
Foundational background source for the SSI/VC vocabulary the paper assumes. Not a differentiator;
cited to anchor terminology (issuer/holder/verifier, the trust triangle) for readers entering from
outside the SSI literature.
