The evaluation lives in one governance context (EU regulation applied to Hungarian administrative
procedure) and one example. Does the metamodel generalize to national eIDAS implementations,
sector-specific governance, and governance traditions whose constraints are **not** reducible to
format-capability requirements?

## Why it matters
A second domain and a non-EU governance source would test external validity and counter the
"single-domain example" limitation. Governance not reducible to format capabilities would require
extending the metamodel beyond annotation markers — a substantive research question.
