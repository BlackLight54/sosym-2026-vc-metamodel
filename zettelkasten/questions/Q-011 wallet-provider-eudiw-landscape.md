How do incumbent wallet and SSO providers (Google, Apple, Meta) approach SSI and EUDIW integration, and
do their platform constraints constitute an additional **governance source** beyond eIDAS/GDPR/W3C?

## Why it matters
External validity for the necessity argument ([[K-010 necessity-of-multilayer-modeling]]): if commercial
platform rules impose format/key/attestation constraints that compete with regulatory ones, they are
further peer governance sources ([[C-032 competing-peer-governance-sources]]) and broaden the catalogue
([[Q-008 broaden-governance-catalog]]) beyond the three currently modeled.

## Status
Open, survey-style. Pairs with the ARF status report ([[S-028 eudiw-arf-status-2026]]), which already
records hardware-key constraints (WSCD, ECDSA P-256) that platform vendors impose in practice.
