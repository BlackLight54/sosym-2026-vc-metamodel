No verified item defines the VC ecosystem concept in consolidated form, and the field keeps saying
so. The gap is an unbroken chain across six years, which turns the advisor's instinct that the
ecosystem concept "deserves a definitional attempt" into a citable gap rather than an intuition
(see [[D-020 rq0-relevance-and-error-taxonomy]]).

## The gap chain, 2020 to 2026

- Schmidt 2021: the SSI ecosystem is "rapidly changing and ill-defined".
- Soltani 2021: surveys the ecosystem while conceding the definition is open.
- Schardong and Custodio 2022: defining SSI is the primary open conceptual problem.
- Laatikainen 2021: SSI ecosystems framed as emergent, prospective socio-technical business ecosystems.
- Richter and Anke 2026: a consolidated framework is still missing as of 2026 (see [[S-051 richter-anke-ssi-framework-2026]]).

## Construction templates for the definitional attempt

The corpus supplies two objects the paper can build its definition from rather than inventing one:

- Otto, ten Hompel, and Wrobel 2022 define data spaces as **designed** ecosystems, a stance that
  fits an object the metamodel constructs rather than observes (see
  [[S-052 otto-designing-data-spaces-2022]]).
- Durumeric 2013 operationalizes the certificate ecosystem as a **measurable object**, a template
  for defining the VC ecosystem as the thing the three layers model.

Combined with Laatikainen's socio-technical framing, these give a low-cost definitional
mini-contribution that fills a documented six-year gap and scopes the design-error taxonomy.

## Caveat: quote precision

Every wording above is paraphrased from abstracts, index records, and verification notes. The
verbatim phrasings, in particular Schmidt's "ill-defined" and Bochnia's "largely unexplored", must
be re-pulled from full texts before any of them enters the paper as a quotation. The gap claim
itself is robust across the chain; only the exact wordings need full-text re-verification at
drafting time.
