Cross-layer constraint interactions are **unpredictable without joint analysis**. C8 (governance
conflict) and C9 (cross-credential gap) are not stated by any single governance source; they *emerge*
when constraints from multiple sources and layers are evaluated together. This is why a checklist or
per-layer review cannot substitute for the integrated model: the interaction, not any individual
constraint, is the error.

This grounds the necessity argument. The contribution is not that each constraint is hard to state, but
that their *joint* consequence is invisible until the layers are modeled together and checked jointly.
