The Reviewer-D pre-emption, and the load-bearing novelty statement. Three points: (1) the VC layers are
**not ontological classification levels** — they are connected by coverage and capability constraints,
not instantiation; (2) cross-layer constraints are **graph predicates over partial models**, not
potency annotations; (3) the contribution is the **constraint formalization across independently
governed sources**, not the layering itself. No prior work combines multi-layer metamodeling with
formalized cross-layer constraints for VC ecosystem design under multi-source governance.
