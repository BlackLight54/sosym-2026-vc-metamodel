Braun et al. (2024) verify an assembled SSI protocol's security with ProVerif. That is protocol
verification of runtime behavior; the present work addresses design-time structural constraints across
metamodel layers. Different questions, different formalisms, **complementary** rather than competing.

S-007 record itself ("SSI, from Specifications to Protocol? Formally Verify Security!",
Braun, Horne, Kafer, Mauw, WWW '24, pp. 1620-1631, CONFIRMED). The Braun-line hypothesis
held; the differentiation above stands unchanged and the corpus map ranks S-007 as the
