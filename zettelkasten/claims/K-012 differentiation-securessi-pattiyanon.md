Both SecureSSI (temporal logic / model checking of SSI architectural patterns) and Pattiyanon et al.
(DSMLs / knowledge graphs for SSI weakness detection) formalize SSI for **security analysis**. Neither
models credential structure across abstraction layers nor formalizes cross-layer governance
constraints. The present work's object is the structural design space under multi-source governance,
not security-property checking.
