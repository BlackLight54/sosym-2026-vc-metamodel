**Decision:** Rename "Claim Property Layer (CPL)" to "Domain Concept Layer (DCL)" throughout the paper.
**Rationale:** Advisor feedback flagged "Claim Property Layer" as non-standard for MODELS reviewers. "Domain concept" is established in multi-level conceptual modeling literature (Atkinson & Kuhne, Gonzalez-Perez, de Lara et al., Kuhne 2006) and immediately recognizable to Foundations Track reviewers.
**Why:** Standard terminology recognized by the target community.
**How to apply:** All references to CPL must use DCL. The three-layer hierarchy reads: Domain Concept -> Credential Schema -> Format-Specific.
