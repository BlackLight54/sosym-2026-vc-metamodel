In the gap paragraph, frame our contribution as "we collected and formalized [constraints from multiple governance sources]" rather than "constraints are scattered across existing tools/standards."

**Rationale (Oszkár's words):** "A gap paragrafban 'scattered across' helyett, összegyűjtöttük és formalizáltuk. Így nem kell cite." — The "scattered across" framing creates citation debt (must cite where they're scattered). "Collected and formalized" positions the formalization itself as the contribution and requires no supporting citations.
