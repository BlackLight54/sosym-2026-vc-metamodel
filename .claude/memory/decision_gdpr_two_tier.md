**Decision:** Frame eIDAS format mandate as hard (SHALL per ARF), GDPR data minimization as operationally binding (cite NAIH 35M HUF enforcement precedent). Scope privacy claim: "if data minimization is to be achieved through technical means at the credential layer, predicate proof capability is necessary."
**Rationale:** GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) requires data minimization in general, not predicate proofs specifically. But NAIH precedent shows enforcement in CSOK context. Two-tier framing lets the conflict stand without overpromising.
**Why:** Honest scoping — don't overclaim GDPR mandates predicate proofs.
**How to apply:** Always distinguish hard regulatory mandate (eIDAS) from operationally enforced principle (GDPR) when discussing the governance conflict.
