**Decision:** Use "family housing subsidy" in all body text. Introduce Hungarian name once in footnote at first mention.
**Rationale:** MODELS is international. "CSOK" carries zero information for non-Hungarian readers. The scenario's value is structural, not geographic.
**Why:** Accessibility for international audience.
**How to apply:** Body text: "the housing subsidy scenario" or "the running example." Footnote: "Based on the Hungarian Family Housing Subsidy (Családi Otthonteremtési Kedvezmény, CSOK), simplified."
