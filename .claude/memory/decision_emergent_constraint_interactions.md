After the gap paragraph, explicitly state that constraints from different governance sources have interaction effects on each other — they show emergent behaviour that cannot be predicted by examining constraints in isolation.

**Rationale (Oszkár's words):** "Ki kell mondani, hogy a kényszereknek van egymásra hatása, emerging behaviour-t, interaction-t mutatnak, és hogy ezt a hatást nem lehet megjósolni, ha nem nézzük őket együtt."

This is the core argumentative move: individual constraints may each be satisfiable, but their joint satisfaction is not guaranteed and cannot be determined without multi-layer analysis. The two headline results (income governance conflict, floor area predicate gap) are instances of this emergent behaviour.
