---
name: "Deliver on claims rather than cut them"
description: "When an audit flags an ungrounded claim, first try to ground it in existing content before cutting"
type: feedback
---

When a claim in the abstract or body appears ungrounded, the default should be to find existing content that supports it and make the connection explicit, not to cut the claim.

**Why:** Martin corrected a plan to remove "community guidelines" from the abstract (audit item PA-B5). AnonCreds, a community-governed specification, was already central to the paper's headline result — the claim just needed grounding, not removal. Martin: "Can we try to deliver on the overpromise?"

**How to apply:** When an audit flags an unsupported claim, check whether existing analysis already covers the concept under a different label before proposing deletion. If the paper's evidence can ground the claim with a small addition (a phrase, a sentence), prefer that over cutting.
